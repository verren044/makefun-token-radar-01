How the implied value is priced

One function scores every row, from the snapshot's own figures — never from a hand-kept list of names. A protocol's implied value is its TVL times the multiple the market already pays for its category, adjusted for growth, maturity, chain breadth and youth.

Category multiples, calibrated from this snapshot

Each multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the live-token protocols in that category. A category with fewer than four live protocols is priced at the global median instead.
